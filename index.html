Company

Advancing Humanity Through Quantum

Founded by physicists and engineers from leading research institutions, Quantum First is dedicated to making quantum computing practical and accessible.

The quantum revolution isn't coming — it's here. But for too long, the power of quantum computing has been locked behind institutional walls, accessible only to a select few with specialized expertise.

We founded Quantum First to change that. Our mission: democratize quantum computing by building the tools, infrastructure, and community that let every developer harness quantum mechanics for real-world impact.

From our labs in Cambridge to our cloud infrastructure spanning three continents, we're pushing the boundaries of what's possible — not just in qubit counts, but in usability, reliability, and the problems we can solve together.
